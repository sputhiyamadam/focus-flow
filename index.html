🌸 Menstrual Cycle Phase Sync

Automatically adapts your recommended daily Pomodoro load and task types around the 4 cycle phases:

  • Menstrual (Days 1–5): Low-cortisol rest, reflection, outlining, & gentle reading blocks.
  • Follicular (Days 6–13): High creativity, brainstorming, coding, & starting new experiments/drafts.
  • Ovulatory (Days 14–17): Peak communication—ideal for committee presentations, outreach emails, & collaboration.
  • Luteal (Days 18–28): Deep detail-oriented focus—finishing revisions, proofreading, figures, & closing checklists.
